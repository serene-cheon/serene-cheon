S.C.

serenecheon@berkeley.edu

I make the invisible visible — the needs, frustrations, and mental models that determine whether a product succeeds or fails.

I graduated from UC Berkeley with a focus on human-computer interaction and cognitive science, which gave me a rigorous, evidence-based lens for understanding how people think, feel, and behave with technology.

Good research, to me, is less about methods and more about curiosity. It's about sitting with ambiguity long enough to ask the right question — and then designing the study that can actually answer it. I've worked across mobile apps, enterprise software, and public-facing services, always with the same goal: making sure the people who matter most have a voice in the room when decisions get made.

Outside of work, I volunteer as an accessibility auditor for non-profit websites, mentor early-career researchers, and spend too much time on Bay Area hiking trails.
