01.1About Serene

Serene approaches research with a sense of steady curiosity: asking why five times, sitting with ambiguity, and trusting that the most interesting answers live one question deeper. She loves spaces that invite nuanced conversations, meaningful narratives, and a high bar for craft.

She graduated from UC Berkeley with a focus on human-computer interaction and cognitive science — a rigorous, evidence-based lens for understanding how people think, feel, and behave with technology. Good research, to her, is equal parts curiosity and rigor.

When she's not in a research session, you can catch her rearranging her bookshelf for the nth time, collecting sea-creature stamps, exploring Bay Area trails, and generally embracing a very spontaneous existence.

If you have an interesting idea, want to collaborate on a project, or just have a chat, please reach out!! She is always on the lookout for new friends and research opportunities. ++ She is available for 2026 opportunities! ☺☺☺
