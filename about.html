About Me

Hi, I'm Serene

UX Researcher. Berkeley grad. Relentless advocate for the people who use our products.

SC

Making the invisible visible

I graduated from UC Berkeley with a focus on human-computer interaction and cognitive science, which gave me a rigorous, evidence-based lens for understanding how people think, feel, and behave with technology.

Good research, to me, is less about methods and more about curiosity. It's about sitting with ambiguity long enough to ask the right question — and then designing the study that can actually answer it. I've worked across mobile apps, enterprise software, and public-facing services, always with the same goal: making sure the people who matter most (the users) have a voice in the room when decisions get made.

Outside of research, I spend time volunteering as an accessibility auditor for non-profit websites, mentoring early-career researchers, and exploring hiking trails in the Bay Area.
