Redesigning Onboarding for a FinTech App

First-time users were abandoning a mobile investment app within the first three screens — but no one knew exactly why. I led a mixed-methods study combining 14 in-depth user interviews with five rounds of think-aloud usability testing to map the emotional and cognitive journey new users experience.

The research revealed a trust deficit: unfamiliar terminology and aggressive data-collection prompts caused anxiety before users had experienced any value. By reordering the onboarding flow, simplifying copy, and deferring optional permissions, the redesign achieved a 34% reduction in early drop-off within six weeks of launch.

Accessibility Audit & Redesign

A consumer app with over two million active users had never undergone a formal accessibility review. I designed and executed a comprehensive audit combining heuristic evaluation against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria, automated scanning, and moderated sessions with seven participants who rely on assistive technologies.

The audit surfaced 47 critical and high-severity issues. Working with engineering in a two-sprint remediation cycle, we resolved all critical issues and brought the app into full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, improving experience scores among assistive-technology users by 61%.

Enterprise Dashboard Research

A logistics company's internal analytics platform had grown organically over five years, resulting in a fragmented dashboard analysts described as "a guessing game." I embedded with two regional operations teams for three weeks, conducting contextual inquiry sessions to observe workflows in their natural environment.

The research identified 12 high-value improvement opportunities — including a critical data-latency issue users had developed workarounds for — and revealed two entirely new use cases, directly informing the next two quarters of the product roadmap.
